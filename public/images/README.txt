PHOTOS FOR THE SALES PAGE
=========================
Upload JPG files into this folder with EXACTLY these names (all lowercase).
A photo that is not here yet simply shows a colored placeholder, nothing breaks.

hero.jpg        Top of the page. Wide landscape, 1920 x 1080, under 400 KB.
                Best choice: a road winding through the volcanic landscape toward
                the sea (a car in the frame is a plus), golden hour. Keep the LEFT
                half calm (sky or sea) because the headline sits there.
sand-dunes.jpg  "A taste of the journey" gallery. 1200 x 800, under 200 KB.
volcano.jpg     Gallery. 1200 x 800, under 200 KB.
tapas.jpg       Gallery. 1200 x 800, under 200 KB.
village.jpg     Gallery. 1200 x 800, under 200 KB.

Tips: save as JPG (not PNG), use your own photos, and compress them first
(for example with squoosh.app) so the page loads fast on a phone.

share.jpg       The picture shown when someone shares the link on WhatsApp, Facebook,
                LinkedIn, etc. 1200 x 630, JPG, under 300 KB. A simple placeholder is
                there now: upload your own file with EXACTLY this name to replace it.
                (Social networks keep old previews for a while; you can refresh them
                with the Facebook Sharing Debugger.)

favicon.svg     The small icon of the browser tab (public/favicon.svg). A simple
                placeholder is there now: replace the file with another SVG of the
                same name. apple-touch-icon.png (180 x 180) is the phone home-screen
                version and favicon.ico the old-browser version.
