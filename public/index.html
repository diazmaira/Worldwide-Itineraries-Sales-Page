About Us

A Different Way to Discover the World

Worldwide Itineraries was created from a simple idea: exploring a place should be about more than just visiting its most famous attractions.

It's about discovering hidden corners, taking the scenic route, finding interesting places and experiencing a destination at your own pace.

We believe that a little planning can open the door to great experiences.

That's why we create carefully planned itineraries that bring together beautiful places, interesting stops and easy-to-follow routes.

Less time planning. More time discovering.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get?

You get a digital, self-guided travel itinerary designed to help you discover a destination at your own pace. Each guide includes a carefully planned route, recommended stops, suggested timings, interesting places to explore, useful links and local tips.

How do I receive my itinerary?

Once your payment is complete, you'll receive an email with a personal access link to your digital itinerary. You can open it directly in your browser on your phone, tablet or computer. No app installation is required.

Your itinerary is available online for 30 days from the date of purchase.

Can I access my itinerary offline?

We recommend downloading the itinerary's offline version before your trip. This way, you can access the essential route information, stops and recommendations even when you don't have an internet connection.

External links, such as Google Maps, may still require an internet connection.

Do I need a car?

It depends on the itinerary. Some routes are designed for exploring by car, while others may be suitable for walking, cycling or using public transport. Check the details of each itinerary before purchasing.

Do I need internet access?

You'll need an internet connection to initially access your online itinerary and open external links. We recommend downloading the offline version and saving any necessary maps before starting your journey.

Can I share my itinerary with others?

Your access link is personal and intended for individual use. Please don't share or distribute it. However, everyone travelling with you can enjoy the journey together using your guide.

I haven't received my email. What should I do?

First, check your spam or promotions folder. If you still can't find it, contact us through the contact form and include your order ID if available. We'll be happy to help.

Do you offer refunds?

As our itineraries are digital products delivered immediately after purchase, refund conditions are subject to our Terms & Conditions and applicable consumer protection laws. If you experience any issues with your itinerary, please contact us and we'll be happy to help.

Need a hand?

Tell us what happened and we'll reply by email.
