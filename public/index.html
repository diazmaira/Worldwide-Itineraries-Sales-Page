Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get?

Self-guided itineraries for the north of Fuerteventura: the route, what to do at each stop, suggested timings, places to eat, direct Google Maps links and local tips.

How do I receive my itineraries?

Right after your payment you get an email with a button that opens your itineraries in the browser. It works on your phone, tablet or computer, and there is nothing to install.

Do I need a car?

Yes. The routes are designed as road trips, so you'll need a rental car or your own vehicle to follow them.

Do I need internet while I drive?

Your itinerary and the Google Maps links need a data connection. Before you set off, you can also save the area for offline use in Google Maps so navigation keeps working in places with weak signal.

Can I share my itineraries with others?

Your access link is personal, so please don't share it. Anyone travelling with you can of course follow the route with you.

I didn't receive my email. What do I do?

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it isn't there, send us a message with the form below and include your order ID if you have it, and we'll sort it out.

Do you offer refunds?

Because the itineraries are digital and delivered instantly, we don't offer refunds. If a place on your route is closed or no longer available, contact us and we'll send you an updated itinerary or another recommendation.

Need a hand?

Tell us what happened and we'll reply by email.
