ITINERARY PHOTOS AND AUDIO
==========================
One folder per itinerary: north, rural, windmills.

  media/north/cover.jpg              Cover photo of the itinerary (1200 x 800)
  media/north/stop-01.jpg            Photo of stop 1 (1200 x 800, under 200 KB)
  media/north/stop-01-en.mp3         Audio for stop 1 in English
  media/north/stop-01-es.mp3         ...in Spanish (de, it, fr work the same way)
  media/north/stop-02.jpg
  media/north/stop-02-en.mp3
  ...

Always use two digits for the stop number: stop-01, stop-02 ... stop-10.

Audio: save as MP3, mono, 64 kbps. That is about 0.5 MB per minute, which keeps
data use low for tourists. Photos: JPG, compressed.

Heads-up: files in this folder can be opened by anyone who knows their exact
address. They are not protected by the purchase link like the itinerary pages
in the "content" folder are.
