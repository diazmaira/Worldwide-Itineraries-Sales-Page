LEGAL TEXTS: edit them here, no code needed.

  terms.md          Terms and Conditions   -> page /terms.html
  privacy.md        Privacy Policy         -> page /privacy.html
  legal-notice.md   Legal Notice           -> page /legal-notice.html

How to update a text:
  1. In GitHub open the file, click the pencil icon (Edit), change the text.
  2. Click "Commit changes". Vercel publishes it by itself in about a minute.

Writing rules (very simple):
  # Title               the page title (one per file)
  ## 1. Section title   a section heading
  ### Sub-heading       a smaller heading
  - text                a bullet
  1. text               a numbered item
  A blank line starts a new paragraph. Lines without a blank line between them stay together.
  Web addresses (https://...) and emails become links by themselves.

Pending placeholders: search the files for "[" to find text still to fill in, for example
[Full legal name], [NIF/NIE], [Business address], [Contact email].
